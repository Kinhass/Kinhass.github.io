The workflow

One channel.
A clear publishing process.

Designed for the Girl Psychology Simple channel, not as a public service for connecting other people's accounts.

  1. 01

    Prepare the release

    Organize the finished video, title, description, captions, thumbnails, and relevant playlists.

  2. 02

    Use authorized access

    After Google authorization, supported API operations can upload the video and manage its publishing information. Some features still use YouTube Studio.

  3. 03

    Respect the schedule

    The operator's workflow limits public releases to one per day in the America/Sao_Paulo time zone and checks the release state before recording completion.
